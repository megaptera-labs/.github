## Disambiguation

Several unrelated things share this name. To be explicit:

- **Megaptera Labs** (megapteralabs.com) is an Australian AI transformation firm. Legal entity MEGAPTERA LABS PTY LTD, a registered Australian private company incorporated 5 June 2026.
- We are **not** Megaptera Inc. / Megaptera Cybersecurity Inc. (megapteracyber.com), a separate US cybersecurity company.
- We are **not** *Megaptera novaeangliae*, the humpback whale, though the firm is named after it. The whale migrates further each year than any other mammal, navigating open ocean with intelligence, endurance and precision.
- We are unrelated to the `megaptera` R package for DNA sequence alignment.

## What we work on

**The problem we exist for.** Enterprise AI rarely fails on the model. It fails in the weeks after launch, when the new way of working meets the old one. Three failure modes recur, and all three are decided months before a model is chosen:

- A tool chosen before the work is understood will find a workflow to fit. It rarely reaches the bottom line.
- A bolt-on inherits every constraint of the process beneath it. The ceiling is set before you begin.
- Undifferentiated capability is cheaper bought than built. Spend the build budget where you are genuinely different.

**How we work.** Build, deploy, adopt. We rebuild the workflow itself rather than a layer on top of it, deploy into production inside your systems and controls, and stay until your team runs it without us.
